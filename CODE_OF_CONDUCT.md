# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Expected behavior:
- Respectful, constructive communication
- Welcoming to contributors of all experience levels
- Focusing feedback on code and ideas, not people

Unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review and respond appropriately, up to and including permanent bans.
